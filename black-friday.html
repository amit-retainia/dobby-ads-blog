Is your listing ready
for Black Friday?

Answer 5 questions. Find out what's holding your listing back.

Is your main image on a pure white background (RGB 255,255,255)?
Amazon's automated checks suppress listings with off-white backgrounds. This is the #1 compliance failure we see.
Are your listing images readable on a phone screen (no tiny text, no dense infographics)?
53% of Black Friday purchases happen on mobile. Images designed for desktop lose more than half your shoppers.
Do you have A+ content live on your listing?
Listings with A+ content convert 5–10% higher. During peak traffic, that gap compounds significantly.
Do you have a product video live on your listing?
Video is the highest-converting asset on a listing. Brands that add video see 2× conversion. Most listings still don't have it.
Was your listing creative last updated in 2025?
Your competitors are refreshing for Black Friday right now. Stale creative loses share to fresh listings every time.
