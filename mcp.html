Setup

Connect in under a minute.

01
Open Claude and go to Connectors

On claude.ai, click your profile, then Settings, then Connectors. On Claude Desktop, click Customize in the left sidebar, then Connectors.

02
Add Dobby Ads as a custom connector

Click +, select "Add custom connector." Enter Dobby Ads as the name and paste the MCP URL:

https://mcp.dobbyads.com/mcp
03
Sign in and start asking

Click Add, then Connect. Sign in with your Dobby Ads account. Every Claude conversation now has access to your agency data.

Order Status

Update Priority

Change any order to Regular, High, or Urgent. Useful when Prime Day, a launch, or a tight deadline shifts your timeline.

"Set order #4521 to urgent. Launch is in 3 days."

Messages and Chats

Send messages directly into an order thread or any of your chat channels. Ask for a revision, post an approval, or check what the team has said.

"Message order #4521: please adjust the background on the main image."

Invoices and Receipts

Pull any invoice by ID or number. Check what's been charged, see payment status, and retrieve Stripe receipts.

Deliverables

Get all deliverables on an order. See what's approved, what's in revision, and what's pending. Pull full deliverable sets when you need the complete picture.

Model Context Protocol is an open standard developed by Anthropic. It lets Claude connect to external tools and data sources through a secure, structured interface. No credentials or raw API access are exposed to the AI.

Dobby Ads MCP works on all Claude plans including Free. Free users are limited to one custom connector. Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans support multiple connectors running simultaneously.
