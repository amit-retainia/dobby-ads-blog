About Dobby Ads

Dobby Ads Inc is an award-winning AI creative agency founded in 2019 in Dover, Delaware, USA. AI-engineered, creatively directed production for Amazon, DTC, and e-commerce brands. 20,000+ projects completed for 450+ brands. 11 international awards across 4 consecutive years.

Services

  • eCommerce Creative — Product images, A+ content, listing assets, storefronts for Amazon, Shopify, and DTC
  • Video & Commercials — Brand films, UGC-style content, social video, TV-ready commercials
  • Animation & 3D — 3D renders, product animations, explainer videos, real estate walkthroughs
  • Branding & Marketing — Brand identity, packaging, Google Ads, Meta campaigns

FAQ

Common questions.

Dobby Ads operates a unified production system across four output categories: eCommerce Creative (product images, A+ content, listing assets, storefronts), Video & Commercials (brand films, UGC content, social video, TV-ready spots), Animation & 3D (3D renders, product animations, explainer videos, walkthroughs), and Branding & Marketing (brand identity, packaging, campaign assets, performance marketing). All creative is produced using AI-powered workflows refined by human creative directors.

Dobby Ads uses AI at every stage of production. It begins with AI-powered competitive visual analysis of your product category. Then the AI pipeline generates 40+ creative directions per project. Human creative directors select and refine the strongest concepts for brand precision, quality, and platform compliance. Direct AI output can look visually striking but often produces oversaturated colours, unrealistic product proportions, and generic compositions that miss marketplace conversion best practices. Our creative directors layer in what AI alone cannot: brand consistency, realistic lighting, and layouts informed by 20,000+ projects of performance data.

Most projects are delivered within 7 to 14 business days. Dobby Ads uses AI-powered production workflows to compress traditional agency timelines without sacrificing quality. Rush delivery is available for time-sensitive launches.

Dobby Ads has built proprietary AI frameworks into its production pipeline, from category research to creative generation to performance validation. Unlike agencies that simply use off-the-shelf AI tools, Dobby's systems are trained on data from over 20,000 completed projects. The output is AI-engineered and creatively directed by a team of senior designers.

You can, and many sellers do. The problem is that direct AI output tends to look generically "AI." You get oversaturated colours, dramatic lighting that doesn't match real product photography, plastic-looking textures, and generic compositions. None of it accounts for Amazon's image guidelines, A+ Content layouts, or what actually drives clicks on a search results page. Dobby uses AI as a starting point, then applies creative direction, brand-specific refinement, and conversion data from 20,000+ projects to deliver output that performs, not just looks pretty.
